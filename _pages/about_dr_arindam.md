### Dr. Arindam Bhattacharjee

**Supervising Faculty**  
*Department of Mechanical Engineering, National Institute of Technology Calicut*

Dr. Arindam Bhattacharjee is a supervising faculty member at the Mechatronics and Robotics Laboratory at NIT Calicut. He mentors research, project development, and academic activities in robotics, control systems, dynamic modeling, and autonomous systems.

#### Research & Supervision Focus
- **Robotic Control & Dynamic Systems:** Non-linear control, state estimation, and dynamic stabilization of robotic platforms.
- **Autonomous Systems & Perception:** Sensor fusion, path planning, and autonomous decision-making for ground and aerial robots.
- **Mechatronic Design:** Integrated mechanical and electronic systems for laboratory automation and industrial applications.
