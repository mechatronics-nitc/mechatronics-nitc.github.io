### Dr. Sudheer A. P.

**Co-founder & Supervising Faculty**  
*Department of Mechanical Engineering, National Institute of Technology Calicut*

Dr. Sudheer A. P. is the co-founder and supervising faculty of the Mechatronics and Robotics Laboratory at NIT Calicut. He guides research and development initiatives spanning robotics, automated assistive systems, kinematics, dynamics of multibody systems, and industrial mechatronics.

#### Research & Supervision Focus
- **Robotics & Autonomous Systems:** Mobile robotics, quadrupedal locomotion, and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).
- **Assistive & Rehabilitation Technologies:** Ergonomic medical devices, automated patient-care positioning systems, and collaborative human–robot interaction.
- **Mechatronics & Automation:** Fluid power electro-hydraulic systems, sensor integration, and real-time robotic control.
