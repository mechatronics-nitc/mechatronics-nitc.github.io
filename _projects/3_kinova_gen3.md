### Overview

The **KINOVA Gen 3 Collaborative Robotic Arm** is an ultra-lightweight, high-precision robotic manipulator engineered for academic research, human–robot collaboration (HRC), and assistive manipulation. With integrated vision and torque sensing at each joint, it provides a safe and versatile platform for advanced robotics research.

### Features & Research Scope

- **Integrated Vision & Sensors:** Embedded high-resolution RGB-D sensor at the wrist enables rapid perception, 3D object detection, and visual servoing.
- **Collaborative Safety:** Torque sensing on all joints allows direct human interaction, compliant control, and gravity compensation modes.
- **Open Architecture:** Full ROS / ROS 2 and API support enabling custom impedance controllers, trajectory generators, and motion planning (MoveIt).
- **Research Applications:** Assistive caretaking, delicate assembly, surgical and rehabilitation robotics experiments, and tele-manipulation.
