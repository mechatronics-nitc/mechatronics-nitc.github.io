### Overview

The **Quadruped Robot** is an advanced four-legged mobile robotic platform designed to navigate unstructured and rough terrains. It serves as a core research platform for studying dynamic locomotion, terrain perception, and autonomous navigation in complex environments.

### Research & Capability Highlights

- **Agile Locomotion:** Capable of trot, crawl, stairs climbing, and dynamic recovery behaviors over uneven surfaces.
- **Autonomous Navigation:** Real-time environment mapping, obstacle avoidance, and path planning using onboard sensors.
- **Perception & Sensing:** Integration with depth cameras, IMUs, and LIDAR sensors for localization and state estimation.
- **Experimental Applications:** Serves as a testbed for control theory, reinforcement learning for quadruped gait adaptation, and inspection tasks in hazardous environments.
