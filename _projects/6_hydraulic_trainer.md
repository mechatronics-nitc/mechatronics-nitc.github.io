### Overview

The **Hydraulic and Electro-Hydraulic Trainer Kit** is an industrial-grade educational and experimental bench designed to provide comprehensive hands-on exposure to fluid power dynamics, industrial hydraulics, and PLC-controlled electro-hydraulic actuation circuits.

### Facility & Training Capabilities

- **Fluid Power Circuit Prototyping:** Quick-connect modular hydraulic manifolds, direction control valves, pressure relief valves, flow regulators, and cylinders.
- **Electro-Hydraulic Control:** Integration with solenoid valves, relay logic, and programmable logic controllers (PLCs) for sequenced automation loops.
- **Instrumentation & Diagnostics:** Analog and digital pressure gauges, flow meters, and sensor feedback for real-time circuit performance monitoring.
- **Curriculum & Practical Learning:** Enables undergraduate and postgraduate students to design, troubleshoot, and validate real-world industrial fluid power systems.
