### Overview

The **Multifunctional Patient-Care Bed** is an innovative healthcare research initiative originating from the Mechatronics Laboratory at NIT Calicut. Designed to address the ergonomics and strain faced by healthcare workers and caregivers, this automated system enables assisted, adaptable repositioning of bedridden patients.

### Innovation Highlights

- **Assisted Repositioning:** Multi-segment articulated surface mechanism engineered to turn, tilt, and shift patients safely without manual heavy lifting.
- **Caregiver Ergonomics:** Drastically reduces physical exertion and musculoskeletal risk for nurses and family caregivers during routine patient care.
- **Pressure Sore Mitigation:** Automated periodic repositioning profiles help redistribute contact pressure points, preventing decubitus ulcers (bedsores).
- **Embedded Control & Safety:** Actuator-driven positioning with soft limiters, intuitive caregiver interface, and patient-comfort compliance.
