### Overview

The **OX-Alpha** is a heavy-duty tracked mobile robot platform developed for rugged outdoor terrain, high traction mobility, and experimental robotic exploration. Tracked locomotion provides superior grip and maneuverability across sand, mud, and obstacles where wheeled platforms struggle.

### Key Capabilities

- **High-Traction Tracked Drive:** Continuous rubber tracks providing low ground pressure and stability on steep inclines and rough surfaces.
- **Autonomous Mobility & Remote Operation:** Dual-mode control system supporting low-latency teleoperation and waypoint-guided autonomous navigation.
- **Payload & Sensor Integration:** Modular deck capable of mounting robotic arms, multispectral sensors, LIDARs, and environmental testing instrumentation.
- **Target Applications:** Search and rescue reconnaissance, outdoor pipeline/infrastructure inspection, and field robotics experiments.
